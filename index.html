About

Greg traded normal for a sailboat named Twig, moving his family of five — plus a dog and a cat — aboard to chase adventure on the high seas. The voyage became And Then We Hit a Rock, a best-selling humor travel memoir about everything going wonderfully wrong.

By day he leads software engineering teams. As a product and technology executive, his focus is on harnessing the collective strengths of engineers to ship innovative software — grounded in coaching, clear communication, and building environments where ideas thrive and good work feels inevitable. Whether it's a codebase or a coastline, the throughline is the same: bring good people together, and go somewhere worth going.
